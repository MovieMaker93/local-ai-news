Alibaba ships Qwen-Image-2.1: a 7B open-weight model that draws in true RGBA

Qwen released Qwen-Image-2.1 on September 20, a compact 7B generator (down from 20B in version 1) that unifies text-to-image and instruction-based editing in one pipeline and is the first major open-weight model to emit real alpha-channel RGBA images from text. It takes up to 10 reference images for composition, runs on a single high-end consumer GPU, and landed day-0 in Diffusers (QwenImage21Pipeline) and ComfyUI. The licence is the catch: weights are restricted to non-commercial/research use, a point that dominated the HN thread more than the architecture.

NemotronLabs VoiceChat: an open full-duplex speech-to-speech model that calls tools

Streaming speech encoder, decoder-only LM, RNN-T transcription branch and streaming TTS in one open-weight architecture with native tool calling. Reports the best pause-handling among evaluated open systems on Full-Duplex-Bench 1.0, 100% takeover after interruptions and 55.1 on VoiceBench.

TierKV: predictive multi-tier KV caching puts on-device long context on a budget

Predicts future KV-cache demand from prefill hidden states before decoding starts, then assigns tokens to exact, low-rank and flash-offloaded tiers via a closed-form solver under device memory and accuracy budgets avoiding both reactive eviction loss and offloading I/O stalls.

arXiv · 2026-09-18

SpecQuant: one base model, three quantizations, speculative decoding that routes by difficulty

Derives INT4, FP8 and FP16 variants from a single shared base model and routes queries by predicted complexity, reserving full precision for hard reasoning. Reports 35-43% speedups on Qwen2.5-based models across three benchmarks with no retraining and no separate draft model.

arXiv · 2026-09-18

An exact error decomposition for W4A4 quantization guides rotations and scaling rules

Splits weight-activation quantization error into an activation-guided compensation term plus an orthogonal residual, turning the analysis into practical rules for Hadamard rotation, sign selection and channel scaling; validated backprop-free across eight Llama and Mistral models.
