RTX 50-series GPU prices spike up to 41% as Blackwell memory costs bite

Tom's Hardware's Newegg median-price tracker shows RTX 50-series cards jumped from June to August: the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB is up 39%, the RTX 5070 up 36%, and the RTX 5090's US median climbed to $4,699.99. Rising GDDR7 memory costs are pushing consumer GPU prices far above MSRP, directly hitting anyone buying cards for a local inference rig.
