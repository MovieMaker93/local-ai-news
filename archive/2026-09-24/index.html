04

UECR-GRPO unifies on-policy distillation and GRPO through entropy-calibrated credit

Integrating verifier and teacher signals in a single GRPO-style update with entropy-calibrated token credit redistribution, the method reaches Avg@12 of 17.21% and 65.09% with Qwen3-1.7B and Qwen3-4B students on five math reasoning benchmarks, beating the strongest baselines at each scale.

arXiv · 2026-09-23

RAMP: adaptive mixed-precision quantization for edge CPU vision models, no GPU needed

Benchmarking 13 layer-sensitivity metrics for INT8 across four networks on two ARM64 platforms, Jensen-Shannon Divergence shows zero catastrophic failures where gradient and weight-statistic metrics fail on up to half of configurations; with K-Means policy allocation it is near-lossless at a 1.81x mean speedup.

arXiv · 2026-09-23

MicroQonv reshapes convolution tensors for efficient microscaling in training and inference

Combining MX quantization with convolution forward/backward passes via channel-batch-first im2col, it cuts activation quantization overhead up to 9x and memory movement up to 7.53x, and enables 4-bit MX latent replay for continual learning at the edge (+5.7% to +11% accuracy).

arXiv · 2026-09-23

Curvature-priced 'quantization price' tables select PTQ configurations before deployment

Formulating weight-space post-training quantization as pre-deployment configuration selection, the work produces a calibration-time price table and a budgeted price-guided selector, making fixed-geometry bit allocation a special case rather than the organizing problem.
