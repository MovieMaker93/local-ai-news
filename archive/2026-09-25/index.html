llama.cpp streams the Qwen3.8-Flash-Next 51B n-gram embedding from SSD at negligible cost

Measured community runs show llama.cpp's default lazy mode serves the 51B n-gram embedding table directly from SSD at only 5-30 MB/s utilization, leaving RAM and VRAM free for quant weights and the KV cache without slowing tokens-per-second. The practical takeaway: a 90 GB UD-Q3_K_XL file effectively needs roughly 55-60 GB of combined RAM+VRAM, and letting llama.cpp auto-place layers beat manual -ngl settings.
