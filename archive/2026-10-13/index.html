Beyond the RAM limit

Janas runs models larger than RAM by streaming MoE experts from SSD

Janas, a pure-C inference engine, keeps mixture-of-experts models larger than system memory viable by streaming experts from the SSD instead of loading everything. It claims 37 tok/s on Qwen3-Next-80B (48 GB) on a 32 GB laptop and beats llama.cpp across the 13 models compared in Q4_K_M — the clearest sign yet that a laptop's RAM ceiling no longer caps what it can run.

Intel formally launches GPU D-Series with the D270 inference card, up to 480GB of LPDDR5X

At the OCP Global Summit, Intel renamed the Crescent Island accelerator as the GPU D270: Xe3P, 1.3 PFLOPS FP8, 1.5 TB/s bandwidth and up to 480 GB of LPDDR5X in a 350 W air-cooled PCIe card. By banking capacity on cheap LPDDR5X instead of scarce HBM, it attacks inference cost-per-gigabyte — a factor that could eventually feed through to cheaper local and hosted inference.

DualQuant: dual-scale, calibration-free weight-and-activation quantization for native 4-bit inference

Quantizes both weights and activations at two scales without needing calibration data, aiming to recover the full throughput gains of low-bit formats on native 4-bit accelerators.

Continually post-training small models via synthetic self-distillation

Shows standard self-distillation fails on small models because of weak in-context ability, and proposes synthetic self-distillation that lets latency-critical small models acquire new skills without forgetting old ones.

arXiv · 2026-10-09

ReadKV: query-adaptive quantization lets each decoding query read the KV cache at the precision it needs

A progressive KV-code scheme that separates the retained-bit budget from the per-query fetch budget, so cheap tokens read low-precision keys and values and important ones read more.

arXiv · 2026-10-08

Adaptive DHiSparse brings speculative decoding to load-adaptive host-memory KV paging

Extends hierarchical sparse KV caches with draft-model speculative decoding and adapts the cache budget to load, targeting long-context serving that runs out of KV memory before GPU throughput does.

arXiv · 2026-10-09

ReplaySSM: cache SSM inputs, not recurrent state

Unrolls state-space recurrence so decode steps replay cached inputs instead of reading and writing state, cutting state I/O and making speculative-decoding rollback cheap in hybrid models.
